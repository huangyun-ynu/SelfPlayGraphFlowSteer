# 保留有效状态的定向修订

Use as optional Director orchestration guidance for a WebShop shopping workflow.

## When

Feedback identifies a specific missing requirement or execution prerequisite after useful shopping work has already been performed.

## Orchestration

Revise the smallest responsibility or dependency that addresses the new gap. Keep the owner's overall acquisition objective intact and pass the current product identity, verified evidence, live option state, remaining budget and unresolved issue into the revision. Invalidate only conclusions affected by the change. Reuse valid work and inspect the new public artifact before making another graph edit.

## Pitfall

Restarting the whole search or narrowing the owner to report-only work can discard useful state and leave acquisition unfinished.

## Boundary

Revisions do not replenish environment actions, transfer ownership implicitly or make stale observations current.
