# 对无进展搜索做局部修订

Use as optional Director orchestration guidance for a WebShop shopping workflow.

## When

Successive searches or revisits consume actions without adding a candidate, evidence or progress on a specific gap.

## Orchestration

Diagnose whether the missing information is catalog coverage, an unexamined variant, a factual property or an unavailable option. Revise the owner's immediate responsibility around one distinguishable uncertainty and an observable result. Preserve the original product intent and useful candidates. Judge progress by what becomes known or feasible, rather than by changed wording alone, and reconsider the remaining completion budget after the revision.

## Pitfall

Paraphrasing the same query or assigning another Agent the same vague search can consume the remaining interaction budget.

## Boundary

Do not impose a fixed retry count, silently change product category, or reset a useful session to create apparent progress.
