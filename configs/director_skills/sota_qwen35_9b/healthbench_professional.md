# HealthBench Professional Director Orchestration Skill

Assign the complete public clinical conversation to a primary response owner. When useful, add a
distinct safety reviewer focused on urgent red flags, uncertainty, contraindications, escalation,
and whether the response directly addresses the user's situation; connect that review to a final
synthesizer or revise the owner using valid evidence. Do not infer private rubrics or physician
answers, and do not include clinical conclusions in delegation prompts. Select a self-contained,
empathetic response that balances actionable guidance with appropriate caution rather than merely
listing risks or disclaimers.
