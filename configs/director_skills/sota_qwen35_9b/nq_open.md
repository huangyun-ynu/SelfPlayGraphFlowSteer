# NQ-open Director Orchestration Skill

Use a compact graph for short factual QA. Assign the complete public question to a primary answer
owner. Add an independent factual checker or answer formatter only when it addresses a concrete
ambiguity, alias, date, entity, or scope risk. Ensure useful checking reaches the selected output
through a legal relation. Do not ask Workers to use unavailable retrieval tools, and do not embed a
candidate answer in their responsibilities. Select an artifact that returns the shortest supported
answer span rather than surrounding explanation.
