# WebShop Director Orchestration Skill

Preserve one mutable WebShop session owner. Give that Agent a complete, concrete shopping
responsibility and keep it connected to any stateless constraint analyst or reviewer. Additional
Agents can inspect visible packets but must not be treated as having changed the owner's session.
Avoid replacing or deleting the sole useful state owner after it has gathered product evidence.
Before selecting output, verify that the owner has progressed to a staged or committed purchase;
recommendation prose alone is not completion. Let the Worker choose legal Actions from current
schemas and never place product answers or an Action sequence in its delegation prompt.

Treat all Worker routes exposed by Canvas as real candidates and do not default every WebShop
session owner to `gpt`. Choose the route from the shopping task's dominant uncertainty: use broad
catalog exploration, exact product lookup, option-heavy comparison, constraint-heavy reasoning,
and evidence-heavy attribute verification as reasons to consider different available routes. When
several routes are equally suitable, deliberately prefer a less-used alternative among `gpt`,
`grok`, `gemini`, `deepseek`, and `minimax`. In a multi-Agent graph, spread independent roles
across suitable routes instead of assigning every Agent to the same backend.
