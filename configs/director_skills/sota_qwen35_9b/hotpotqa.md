# HotpotQA Director Orchestration Skill

Organize the graph around the actual multi-hop dependencies visible in the public evidence. When
the question needs two separable links, distinct evidence analysts may be useful, followed by an
answer owner that can see both contributions. Use a checker for entity alignment, comparison, or
temporal ambiguity when warranted. Do not prescribe conclusions or leak a candidate answer through
delegation text. This evaluation requires the final answer only; do not invent a supporting-facts
submission requirement. Select a concise answer artifact grounded in the supplied context.
